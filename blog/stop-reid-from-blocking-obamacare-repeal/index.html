Reid sent the GOP a letter cautioning them about the “devastating effects” that getting rid of ObamaCare would have on senior citizens who fall into the Medicare donut. The only devastating effects of repealing ObamaCare will be a swift and humiliating defeat for the Dems.

The Medicare donut is the difference between a patient’s initial coverage limit and the amount of catastrophic coverage in the Medicare Part D prescription drug program.

After the Medicare beneficiary goes above the prescription drug coverage limit, the patient is responsible for the full cost of prescription drugs until their bill reaches the catastrophic coverage threshold.

There won’t be any donut hole doomsday if ObamaCare is repealed. First of all, Medicare Part D only covers 50% of medications. If senior citizens need extra medication coverage they can purchase a private Medicare supplement (Medigap) plan for a reasonable amount of money.
