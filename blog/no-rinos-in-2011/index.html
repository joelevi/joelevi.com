No RINOs in 2011!

December 14, 2010 · Articles

Friends of Liberty,

The battle to reclaim our constitutional rights being stolen day after day by Barack Hussein Obama and his Marxist thugs isn't over now that Republicans have taken control of the U.S. House of Representatives and made marked gains in the Senate. In fact, it's only just begun.

HOLD THEIR FEET TO THE FIRE - SELECT HERE TO PETITION CONGRESS AND DEMAND THEY UPHOLD CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES!

The temptations in the halls of Congress and throughout Washington D.C. are enormous -- for many even of good intentions, the siren songs of power and privilege are simply overwhelming. Remember the capitulations of the 1980s? Or how the 1994 "Contract with America" was betrayed in less than two years? My friend, we must not let this happen again. We must be bold in the peoples' cause. We must hold the newly elected Republicans' feet to the fire -- and, as necessary, be prepared to put Old Bull Elephants out to pasture!

We, the people, voted the 112th Congress into office on the promise that they will promote constitutional principles and repudiate Obama socialism. Rep. John Boehner, the new Speaker of the House, and all the GOP "leadership" must be held to this same bedrock constitutional standard. The entire Class of 2010 needs to understand that they are ON PROBATION. The 112th Congress is morally and constitutionally obligated to REPEAL ObamaCare and STOP the SPENDING!

And delivering that message is what Declaration Alliance PAC, with your help, is doing -- even throughout this Holy Christmastide Season and the festive New Year!

HOLD THEIR FEET TO THE FIRE - SELECT HERE TO PETITION CONGRESS AND DEMAND THEY UPHOLD CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES!

Mr. Boehner is hearing loud and clear from DA PAC that we will accept NO compromise with Barack Obama and his socialist cronies. We demand the repeal of ObamaCare and its oppressive requirements. We insist that taxes must be cut and spending must be slashed. The unconscionable profligacy of the federal government MUST NOT be subsidized by tax increases upon the groaning backs of the productive citizens of our nation.

The newly-elected members of the Class of 2010 must be told -- in no uncertain terms -- to do in Washington what we, the people, sent them to do . . . and that begins with repealing the draconian, unconstitutional ObamaCare!

YOU are the key to making this happen.

The truth is, your involvement today will help guarantee we don't suffer the same retreat from constitutional principles we've witnessed in previous Republican electoral victories. The ultimate success of this recent election -- whether it will go down in history as a great awakening, a watershed moment for liberty and freedom, or end in compromise and defeat as have so many elections before -- depends on what you choose to do today.

First and foremost, I need you to SELECT HERE to sign the enclosed "Petition to Rep. John Boehner." This is your way of demanding he work with, and support, the Tea Party-endorsed candidates who will serve as freshmen legislators under his leadership.

Congressman Boehner must know that compromise with Obama and his socialist faction is unacceptable, and that a return to the "good old days" and so-called "bi-partisan business as usual" with GOP acquiescence to leftist pressure will not be tolerated.

As I write this, the Republicans are selecting Committee Chairs, and Caucus Leaders -- the signs are unsurprising but disturbing that without unremitting pressure from Constitution-minded patriots like YOU and relentless lobbying by principled patriot organizations like DA PAC, Congress will be back to their regular "fool the voters" shenanigans in no time!

A great opportunity lies before us, and that opportunity must not be squandered. You must help DA PAC convince Mr. Boehner that the failures of the past cannot be repeated.
