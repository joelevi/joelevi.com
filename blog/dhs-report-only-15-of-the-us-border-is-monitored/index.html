DHS Report: Only 15% Of The US Border Is Monitored

March 7, 2011 · Articles

Napolitano Failing On Homeland Border Security; Obama’s Federal Government Is Not Doing Its Job To Secure the Border

ALERT: Department of Homeland Security (DHS) Secretary Napolitano says that the U.S. southern border is “more secure than ever before.”  She cannot be serious! A recent report released by her own DHS states that Homeland Security is only “achieving varying levels of operational control of 44% of the southwest border.”

 

Ms. Napolitano has set low standards for herself.   In the rest of the United States 44% is an “F” -- that's a failing grade.  But only politicians and bureaucrats in Washington tout a truly sad statistic and believe that the BEST this country can achieve in securing its own border is “44% operational control” -- when that's not even halfway acceptable.

What is “operational control” anyway?  A few more pages into the DHS report shows exactly what “operational control” is. Whenever they refer to “operational control” they are in fact only referring to places on the border where there is even a DHS attempt to monitor.

The DHS report goes on to say that only 15% of the border is actually defined as “controlled.” In other words, ONLY 44% of the actual border is even on the plate for security, and of that 44% ONLY a measly 15% is actually an area where an illegal alien has a “high probability of apprehension.”

So in reality, of the nearly 2,000 miles of U.S border that the DHS is tasked with defending only 129 miles of it are actually controlled. 129 MILES! That is almost the same as the border of the state of Rhode Island! The entire “might” of the U.S. Government can’t even secure the border of its SMALLEST STATE!  I also means they are only counting the illegals crossing 129 miles of the border therefore as many as 85% of illegals that cross are not counted and we must rely on honest illegals to fill our US census information.

To add to this outrage the new DHS budget also reveals that Napolitano and the Obama administration have absolutely no funding set aside to expand border security, and that is true for the next TWO years. So, for 24 more months not one single dime will be spent on securing more than 129 miles of the entire 2,000 mile southwest border!

This is more than incompetence, this is sabotage. Obama and his administration have failed to lead, failed to protect Americans and failed to uphold their Constitutional oath to enforce the laws of the United States of America. Their betrayals are a threat to the nation and their duplicity directly insults the intelligence of the American people.

Moreover, it is proof that Obama and his Chicago Way gang are intentionally ignoring their responsibility to defend the citizens of the United States from foreign invasion and criminal penetration, and secure the border! They are literally spending $3 BILLION a year to accomplish NOTHING!

You should not be happy with 44% "operational control." The federal government has failed to do its job at securing the border and are even intentionally sabotaging any feasible strategy to effectively expand border control by refusing to fund border security operations.

Stand with them and support their fight for freedom and a nation of laws! They aren’t giving 44% they are giving 110% to secure you and your family!
