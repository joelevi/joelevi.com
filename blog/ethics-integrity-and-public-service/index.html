Ethics, Integrity, and Public Service

September 26, 2010 · Articles

Sutherland Institute is pleased to invite you to our next Transcend Series session on Thursday, September 30, 2010 - "Ethics, Integrity, and Public Service" - to be facilitated by our friend and colleague, Dr. Quinn McKay.

Following check-in and seating at 8:30 am, the session will begin at 9:00 am and conclude at 3:00 pm. The venue will be the Sutherland Institute Conference Room located at 307 West 200 South, Suite 5005, in downtown Salt Lake City, Utah.

There is no charge for this event and limited free parking is available in the Crane Building's south parking lot and also along the street and in the pay lot just south of us ($5 for the day). Light snacks will be provided throughout the day. There will be a lunch break on your own - many convenient eateries are located nearby.

Seating is reserved. To register, please send an email to si@sutherlandinstitute.org or call our office at 801-355-1272. If we don't already have your full contact information, you will be asked to provide it when you register as we will be emailing preparatory material to you in advance.

Dr. McKay will refer to his most recent book, The Bottom Line on Integrity, in the course of his presentation. Copies of the book will be available for purchase, if you desire, on the 30th.

The sessions of our award-winning, three-part Transcend Series are stimulating, challenging, and enlightening. We encourage you to find time in your busy schedule to join us. We look forward to seeing you on Thursday, the 30th.

Warm regards,

Paul T. Mero, President
Sutherland Institute
