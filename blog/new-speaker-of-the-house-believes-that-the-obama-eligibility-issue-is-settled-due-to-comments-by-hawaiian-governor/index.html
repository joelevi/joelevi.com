New Speaker of the House believes that the &quot;Obama Eligibility&quot; issue is &quot;SETTLED&quot; due to comments by Hawaiian Governor

January 10, 2011 · Articles

John Boehner is the new Speaker of the House for the 112th Congress.

In spite of his staunch patriotic, conservative stand, he has one concept TOTALLY WRONG.

He believes that the "Obama Eligibility" issue is "SETTLED" due to comments by Hawaiian Governor Neil Abercrombie, who claims he knew Mr. Obama and his parents when Obama was growing up---in Hawaii.

Boehner aside, it is now time that the Members of the U.S. Congress finally conduct the Congressional hearings conservatives have promised America for two years---to answer the "Is he, or is he not..." question, regarding the eligibility of Barack Hussein Obama to serve as the President of the United States of America.

We've fought this fight for two years. We have phrased the question every way that it can be asked! NOW, it's time for the answer.

However, how can we remove him from office when the Speaker of the House says it is already "settled." Please stand with me during this struggle for the truth.

Now that the 112th Congress is now in session, we face a serious problem and dilemma. Speaker of the House John Boehner actually believes liberal Hawaiian Governor Neil Abercrombie when he says that Mr. Obama was actually born in Hawaii. According to John Boehner: the Eligibility issue is settled.

Over the past two years, because Congress failed to "vet" Barack Hussein Obama's eligibility before the Electoral College voted (even though they vetted Senator John McCain), several American citizens have filed lawsuits, believing that the federal judiciary would protect the Constitution of the United States, which they swore to uphold. Only, instead, these federal judges told the plaintiffs in these actions that this was not a "judicial matter" but, rather, they said, it was a "Congressional matter." Congress, the courts said, need to answer that question. The 1st Amendment to the U. S. Constitution guarantees its citizens the right to petition its government for a redress of grievances. To date, the voice of the People has been ignored (except during the Election of 2010). It is now time that the People are heard in the matter of the eligibility of Barack Hussein Obama to hold the position of Chief Executive of the United States of America.

Let's look at what needs to happen and what questions need to be asked:

  • Congressional hearings as to Mr. Obama's eligibility to serve as U.S. President.
  • Congressional hearings as to Mr. Obama's eligibility to be a U.S. Senator.
  • Article 2 of the U.S. Constitution requires that the President must be a natural born citizen. Why did the U.S. House of Representatives and the U.S. Senate NOT pursue Barack Obama's eligibility---but did "vet" U.S. Senator John McCain?
  • Why did the federal courts "stonewall" the various lawsuits that were brought before them---even the USJF case of Drake v. Obama---as to the eligibility of Barack Obama to serve as President? Or the case of Kerchner, et al, v Barack Hussein Obama, in support of which USJF submitted an amicus ("friend of the court") brief? The American people, as a whole, have been stonewalled on this issue for two years. It's now time for answers!

Why have the federal courts NOT adequately addressed a single citizen's grievance concerning Mr. Obama's eligibility?

To end the quest for proof that he was eligible to seek the office of President of the United States, all Mr. Obama had to do was produce his original long-form, physician-issued, birth certificate. That would have satisfied YOU and me. All Mr. Obama has to do is show his original long-form birth certificate.

But no---

Instead, he has spent, according to published reports, nearly TWO MILLION DOLLARS in hiding not only his birth certificate and grade school records---but all of his personal records. Freedom-loving patriots, who adhere to the United States Constitution, consider this an absolute outrage. We demand a Congressional investigation! It's time!

Worse, over the last two years, Obama and the far-left media has done a very good job of helping Mr. Obama hide the truth, by posting a copy of a "Certification of Live Birth" (which merely affirmed that the State of Hawaii acknowledged a claim that Barack Obama was born, somewhere in the world, on August 6, 1961,and that his family intended for him to be a resident of Hawaii); and by attempting to intimidate anyone who raises questions as to why Mr. Obama has spent so much money to conceal his records if he has nothing to hide. Yet, recent polls suggest that up to 58 percent of the American people now wonder where Mr. Obama was born. None of those who accept Obama's paperless version of his birth ask why, if he was born in the United States, and not in what is now Kenya, has he spent so much money concealing that fact? A two million dollar legal and PR campaign to hide the truth can trigger a lot of doubt.

NOW, IT'S TIME FOR THE TRUTH!

USJF currently has two cases in California that are still before the Courts---one before the California State Supreme Court and one before the United States 9th Circuit Court of Appeals.

Mr. Obama has not been forthcoming with any of the documents which would shed much light on his early years. When you peruse the following list, you will realize one thing: you wouldn't spend two million dollars to conceal this information from media scrutiny, unless you had something to hide.

  • Kindergarten records
  • Punahou school records
  • Occidental College records
  • Columbia University records
  • Harvard Law School records
  • Passport
  • Medical records
  • Files from his years as an Illinois state senator
  • Illinois State Bar Association records
  • Baptismal records
  • Adoption records

Yet, he has spent nearly $2 million, according to published reports, to HIDE these records from the American people!

On the lighter side, as Mr. Obama's popularity nosedived a month before the midterm elections, Tonight Show host Jay Leno quipped that "... things are so bad for Barack Obama these days that even Democrats are asking for his birth certificate."

It may be a joke---to some. But it's extremely serious to those of us who understand Article II of the U.S. Constitution. Please help USJF in this critical Constitutional battle.

We need Congress to push for Congressional hearings to settle the question of Mr. Obama's eligibility once and for all. The American people have a right to know if the person sitting in the Oval Office is, indeed, legally holding the of Office of the President of the United States. The voters of the United States needs these hearings to take place---not "whitewash," "show" hearings, but a real investigation of the issue, to get at the truth. That is what the American people want. And, that's what the U.S. Constitution demands. And, what's more, that what the Founding Fathers of this great nation would expect.

RIGHT NOW, we have just completed the second and third of THREE videos that will soon be aired nationally, discussing the Barack Obama eligibility issue. This is costing USJF TENS OF THOUSANDS OF DOLLARS to produce and run these programs nationwide, so we really do need your financial assistance! AND, we need it NOW!

Please assist the United States Justice Foundation in this all-out effort to finally get the answer to the question that all of us---even the skeptics---have asked: where was Barack Hussein Obama really born?

I need to know that you are shoulder-to-shoulder with us in this "final stand" against those who seek to destroy the U.S. Constitution. This is a quest for honest answers, and we must not be deterred from getting at the truth.
