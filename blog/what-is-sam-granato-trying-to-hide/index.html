What is Sam Granato trying to hide?

October 28, 2010 · Articles


Dear Friend of Liberty,

What is Sam Granato trying to hide?

You see, several weeks ago, I sent surveys to all the candidates in Utah’s U.S. Senate race.  As of today, Sam Granato still refuses to respond to our survey.

He refuses to let you know where he stands on the important issues facing Utah.

We already know he supports ObamaCare and thinks it “was a start, but we still have a long way to go…”

With this catastrophe, health insurance rates are going to sky rocket and could cost Utahans their jobs.

But what isn’t Sam Granato telling us?

We don’t know how he will vote on issues like gun control or PATRIOT Act reauthorization.

We don’t know if he supports a thorough audit of the Federal Reserve - something supported by 80% of the American people.

He won’t say exactly where he stands on taxes.  Right now is the wrong time to raise taxes on the hard working people of Utah, who are struggling to get by.

He won’t tell us if he will be in the back pockets of labor unions by supporting Card Check legislation, which will take away the secret ballot in union elections.

And he won’t explain where he stands on spending.  We don’t know whether he will continue Washington’s out of control ways or reign in spending and not mortgage our children and grandchildren’s futures.

That’s why it’s vital you call Sam Granato today at 801-486-2010. Demand he return his survey in support of liberty and limited government and apologize for his support of big government programs like ObamaCare.

Also, please call Mike Lee at 801-784-0052 and urge him not to back down from his clear positions against the freedom- and economy-wrecking schemes that Sam Granato supports.

Mike Lee is the only top tier candidate in this race to answer in favor of individual freedom and limited government.

Mike Lee knows the economy grows and you have more freedom when the government stays out of your way and you get to keep more of your money.

You can view all of the results from both the U.S. Senate and U.S. House here.  Contact Utah’s U.S. House candidates listed on the linked roster who have not yet responded to our survey and urge them to stand up for liberty and limited government.

These 2010 elections are critical for the future of our economy.

Our fragile economy is cracking under the weight of too much spending, too much taxation, and too much government involvement.

It’s up to you and me to put a stop to it all.

I can’t do it alone.  So if you can afford to, please make a generous contribution of $100, $50, $25, or $10 in support of Campaign for Liberty’s critical 2010 programs.

But most importantly, please contact Democrat Sam Granato, and the rest of Utah’s candidates, IMMEDIATELY.

Sincerely,

John Tate, President
Campaign for Liberty

P.S.  Call Mike Lee today and urge him to continue standing up for individual freedom and limited government.

Then contact his major opponent, Sam Granato, and ask him what he is trying to hide by not answering Campaign for Liberty’s survey.  Demand he apologize for his past support of bigger government and return his survey in support of liberty and limited government.

P.P.S.  Please also contact Utah’s U.S. House candidates listed on the linked roster who have not yet responded to our survey and urge them to stand up for liberty and limited government TODAY.
