HealthCare Rates Up 30% after ObamaCare

January 7, 2011 · Articles

Congress Votes January 12th To Stop ObamaCare-Apocalypse!

ALERT: ObamaCare has imposed new taxes on drug makers, restrictions on tax-free medical spending and caused colossal rate hikes on healthcare insurance premiums!

Repeal ObamaCare - SEND FAXES TO ALL 535 CONGRESSMEN!

Because ObamaCare eliminated things like pre-existing exclusions, no cost sharing for preventative services, and limits on deductibles, every American's insurance premium will increase by 30%. That's a lot, considering household income has fallen 2.9% since the start of the Recession.

The details of ObamaCare are not even complete and it doesn't officially roll out until 2012 yet Americans and businesses across the country are already being torpedoed. On January 1st, two of the law's 19 tax hikes took effect. ObamaCare's excise tax on drug manufacturers is already pushing brand-name drug prices through the roof.

The tax forces drug companies to cough up huge amounts of cash to the government to pay for ObamaCare's $1 trillion dollar price tag. Will the punishing tax hikes help offset the healthcare laws cost for consumers?
No. Because these drug companies are private sector businesses that haven't been snapped up by the Obama machine, they still have to make a profit.

Now, because drug manufacturers are forced to distribute their profits into government coffers, they're simply raising the drug prices for regular Americans. Many people have had the price of their medications increase and most Americans will see their drug prices increase by the end of this year.

Repeal ObamaCare - SEND FAXES TO ALL 535 CONGRESSMEN!

Two large drug companies, Genentech and Allergan, have already informed hospitals that they will stop selling discounted drugs and increase their prices. Keep in mind, ObamaCare is hiking up drug prices in the midst of a Recession with a 9.8% unemployment rate.

The second part of the ObamaCare taxation scheme is directed toward consumer-driven insurance plans like health savings accounts (HSAs), flexible spending accounts (FSAs), and health reimbursement accounts (HRAs). These plans work like a debit card. A plan holder puts a certain amount of money into the "card" (i.e. plan) every year and spends it as needed.

These plans allow consumers to be in directly in charge of the money in their healthcare accounts. At the end of the year, the money that is not spent is rolled over into the following year. Consumer driven plans encourage their holders to strive to live a healthier lifestyle and be more mindful of their healthcare spending, something that ObamaCare has certainly ignored.

Repeal ObamaCare - SEND FAXES TO ALL 535 CONGRESSMEN!

After January 1st, FSA account holders are facing new restrictions on over-the-counter medications, along with doubled penalties for paperwork screw-ups. That means you can no longer use your FSA account to buy a Tylenol for that ache in your back. Instead, you'll have to reach into your own wallet to purchase it.

If you have the nerve to cross the healthcare police and use your FSA account to purchase that bottle of Tylenol, you'll be slapped with a 20% tax penalty from the from the friendly guys at the IRS. That's not all. On January 1st, the withdrawal tax on health savings accounts increased from 10% to a whopping 20% to feed the pay for ObamaCare's $938 billion deficit.

Because only 22.7% of American's hold consumer-insurance driven plans, many Americans think they're safe from the claws of ObamaCare. Think again. The Heritage Foundation estimates employee-sponsored insurance coverage will decrease by 8 million people after 2012 and companies will be less likely to hire new employees that will increase the cost of doing business.

Since ObamaCare shredded our Constitution and now it's required by law that people purchase government healthcare, employers can't refuse to pay for it, and they'll simply start laying people off.
