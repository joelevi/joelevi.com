Enough is enough! Republicans must make Congress execute their Congressional authority and oversight of the FCC. They need to rescind the FCC vote because the FCC does not have the Congressional or Constitutional authority to expand governmental regulation.

These laws gag critical opinions, corruption exposes and speech that protest their government. The communist country employs 50,000 people who act as internet police, scouring the internet for offenders to punish.

Comments on blogs and internet forums that question Chinese propaganda are wiped out within minutes by the internet police. I can just imagine Obama appointing a new internet czar and thousands of new anti-free speech government workers to police the internet.

The new regulations will not only destroy free speech, but will also annihilate capitalism. Right now, the internet is the last true laissez-faire form of capitalism in our country. People can buy and sell what they want with little interference from the government.

The new communist-style internet regulations will deter innovation, raise operating costs and ultimately raise consumer prices.
