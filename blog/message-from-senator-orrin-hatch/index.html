Message from Senator Orrin Hatch

May 18, 2010 · Articles

Note:

Our Precinct does not endorse any particular or specific Candidate. However, if you are interested in learning about them and what they stand for, we’re relaying this information. The following may contain the opinions of a candidate and is not necessarily the opinion of the Precinct. This message may have been written by the candidate and should be attributed as such. Please contact the candidate directly with questions.

Fellow Utahn --

This week, the recently passed, unconstitutional health care law was back in the news and I was once again leading efforts to highlight its problems.

On Tuesday, the Congressional Budget Office (CBO) revealed that ObamaCare will cost $115 billion more than Democrats told us -- pushing the total cost over $1 trillion. I discussed the fiscal insanity of the health care law on Fox News with Greta Van Susteren and on the Fred Thompson Radio Show this week.

I also joined Governor Herbert in writing an op-ed in the Deseret News on the importance of protecting and defending federalism.

This week's update also features my op-ed in the St. George Spectrum on the importance of ensuring we take care of Utahns harmed by our government's nuclear testing, and an editorial from the Ogden Standard-Examiner highlighting benefits of a bill I am co-sponsoring that will help bring much needed transparency to the congressional earmark process.

Sincerely,

Senator Hatch

P.S. Have you seen the video I showed at the Republican convention yet? If you haven't, please take a few minutes to catch up on what I have been up to.
