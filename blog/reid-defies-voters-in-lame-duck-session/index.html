Are you and your family prepared for the legislative tsunami that Congress is gearing up to strong-arm once they get back from vacation? If not, you better grab your raincoat and head for high ground because this storm is going to be a doozy.

Just when you thought things couldn't get any worse, the Dems have announced they will smother our country with 20 disastrous new bills. It's government gone wild in Washington. It is critical we block the passage of these bills before it's too late. We must take advantage of our window of opportunity and defend our country against this madness!

Lame Duck Legislative Bomb Plans Exposed:

  • The largest tax increase in history
  • The DREAM Act (i.e. amnesty for illegals)
  • Repeal of "Don't ask, don't tell"

And that's not all. Obama will use the lame duck session to confirm five radical judges to the federal court. He's already stuffed two extreme ideologues into the Supreme Court. Enough is enough.

Gangster Government Plans On Passing 20 Bills 'ObamaCare Style'

It's no secret, we are living in scary times. It seems like ages ago when we had representatives who proposed sane bills and passed them without resorting to bullying tactics.   • KILLING THE BUSH TAX CUTS: 23 million hardworking Americans will give Obama and his cronies an additional $3,400 in taxes. This will be the largest tax increase in American history. You have to wait until next year to find out if YOU are one of the unlucky ones to get a surprise tax hike.
