New tax found in ObamaCare: 3.8% on unearned income

January 19, 2011 · Articles

Did you know that beginning January 1, 2013, ObamaCare imposes a 3.8% Medicare tax on unearned income, including the sale of single family homes, townhouses, co-ops, condominiums, and even rental income?

ANY INCOME generated from sources other than employment---which could be real estate transactions, inheritance, rental income, interest, annuities, etc.---will be taxed starting in 2013.

The more we find out about ObamaCare, the WORSE IT GETS for the American people! Socialized medicine comes at a PRICE---and WE THE PEOPLE WILL PAY THAT PRICE. CLICK HERE if you are unwilling to allow the government to destroy our healthcare system, while forcing you to pay for that destruction! DEMAND THE REPEAL OF OBAMACARE TODAY! The House plans to vote on the repeal of ObamaCare on Wednesday, January 19th. Make your voice heard right now!

The Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act (PPACA) is over 2,000 pages of job-killing, big government hogwash that gives bureaucrats more control over your life and intrudes into the most personal decisions you and your family can make.

Nobody read it, and the debate wasn't televised on C-Span, as promised. We got hit over the head with Nancy Pelosi's big gavel, and on March 23, 2010, one signature destroyed healthcare in America as we know it.

It raised our taxes, it reduced the quality of care, it strained our resources AND it is hurting the economy. Karen Campbell of the Heritage Foundation says the Medicare tax "would reduce demand for investment, which is the last thing that the economy needs right now. It would slow [economic] recovery, reduce employment opportunities and hinder wage growth."

Instead of being fiscally responsible and slashing reckless spending, the Obama administration is just coming up with new ways to tax us so they can pay for their big government programs!

Look at how poorly Congress manages government---do you want these people taking over your family's medical care? Would you put anything in the hands of the government and expect it to survive?

They LIED to you when they claimed rates would go down... they LIED to you when they claimed all children would be covered... they LIED to you when they claimed you could keep your current plan... they LIED to you when they claimed you would see no increase in your taxes.

Now they are LYING again about how they are going to pay for it.

They said there were no Death Panels, but that was a LIE, too.

Obama didn't want a public horrified by Death Panels to kill his healthcare takeover, so he scrapped most of it from the bill, then quietly implemented new Medicare "regulations" that amount to Death Panels. An Independent Medicare Advisory Board is looking for ways to cut Medicare costs, which means THEY will make the decisions about your medical care that you and your doctor normally would.

Enough with the outright lies... enough with the spin... enough with the talking points. Americans do not want socialized medicine in America! We have a very real chance to repeal ObamaCare RIGHT NOW with the GOP-led House. They are voting on Wednesday, January 19th, so you must make your voice heard NOW. We have the momentum! We can REPEAL ObamaCare this week. FAX CONGRESS RIGHT NOW!

Obama is killing the the best healthcare system in the world, and he is using your money to kill it.

They are banking on our silence. So it is critically important that we keep the momentum for repeal going.

The American people rejected ObamaCare in November, and now we must put the final nail in the coffin of socialized medicine here in America. Fax Congress RIGHT NOW and demand they immediately repeal ObamaCare! Don't wait until your taxes go up and your quality of healthcare decreases---SEND YOUR FAXES RIGHT NOW!
