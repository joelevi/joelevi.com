This is a very simple plan. We intend to grind Obama down. We will wear him out. And, make no mistake, THAT is his Administration’s worst fear. The “Chicago Mob” in the White House faers that the will of the American people is stronger and more enduring than their Radical Socialist Agenda. ObamaCare Is On The Ropes.  Obama Is Already Giving Up Ground.

The New York Times, THE newspaper of the Liberal Left grudgingly published on January 2nd 2011: "The Obama administration, reversing course, will revise a Medicare regulation to delete references to end-of- life planning as part of the annual physical examinations covered under the new health care law, administration officials said Tuesday."

Rush Limbaugh in his usual searing style summed it up like this: "The White House apparently has flip-flopped again. Last week they were caught trying to sneak in the death panels, essentially. A federal rule that Medicaid doctors, in order get paid by Medicaid, Medicare, had to have end-of-life discussions with their seasoned citizen patients once a year."

The Obama Administration did not do this about-face on this “death panel” policy out of moral decency.  If ObamaCare survives, mark our words, you'll see it again.

The last time they pursued an “End of Life” policy, former Alaska Governer Sarah Palin called it “death panels,” and she was followed up by then Minority Leader Boehner who said that the policy would “start us down a treacherous path towards government-encouraged euthanasia.”

The policy was scrapped from the version of ObamaCare that passed Congress but some how resurrected itself after passing the House. The New York Times reported "The proposed rule, published for public comment in July, did not include advance care planning." In other words the “end of life” policy did NOT appear in the proposal that was available to the public. It was slotted in afterwards... IN SECRET.

And then there were sinister attempts to keep the policy from being made public. News reports at the time carried the following transcript from Congressman Earl Blumenauer in an email to supporters of “end of life” policy:

“While we are very happy with the result, we won’t be shouting it from the rooftops.... We would ask that you not broadcast this accomplishment out to any of your lists, even if they are ‘supporters’ — e-mails can too easily be forwarded.... Thus far, it seems that no press or blogs have discovered it, but we will be keeping a close watch and may be calling on you if we need a rapid, targeted response. The longer this goes unnoticed, the better our chances of keeping it.”

Why the smoke and mirrors? Why the sinister attempt to keep the public in the dark? And why the sudden reversal? Despite the crazed babbling coming from the bleatingsheeple on the Left, the answer is really quite simple. Obama and his cronies know thatObamaCare is balanced upon on the edge of a blade, if it waivers but an inch it will come crashing down.

That's why they scrapped the policy... but more to the point, when it comes toObamaCare, that's why the liberal elites in Washington and in the media are counting on your silence. Their precious ObamaCare is on the ropes.  They know your continued outrage will kill it and that's why now is the time for the American people to rain down punches and knock it out for good.

If At First You Don't Succeed. Lie, Lie Again.

Before the “end of life” policy was reversed the spin doctors on the left were frantically trying to control the damage. As you read earlier, Congressman Blumenauer’s email promised a “rapid, targeted response.”

And that is what the liberal spin-doctors delivered. They played fast and loose with the truth... they did it multiple times... and they are STILL doing it.

First they tried to tell us: 'We didn't do it... George W. Bush did it.' And The New York Times, like the faithful liberal lap dog that it is, reported “The White House on Sunday said the new Medicare directive, reported Sunday by the New York Times, doesn't constitute a resurrection of the original health-care-bill language. It said the George W. Bush administration had already put in place guidelines allowing for Medicare to pay for end-of-life consultations.”

Baloney.... In actuality, Congress passed a revamp to Medicare in 2008 which simply permitted doctors to discuss "end-of-life" options with patients during their INITIAL"welcome to Medicare visit." President Bush VETOED the bill, but Congress overrode Bush's VETO. So... no... 'Bush didn't do it,' and moreover, there's a huge difference between simply PERMITTING doctors to brief patients on Living Wills ONCE andENCOURAGING them to do it each and every year.

Betsy McCaughey, the former Lieutenant Governor of New York said; "Government should never prescribe what is discussed between doctor and patient, or pressure doctors financially to push their patients into living wills and advanced directives."

And once these liberal manipulators realized that they couldn’t win that argument, they moved on. Now these desperate lefties are telling us that the REPEAL ofObamaCare will INCREASE the federal deficit. More ridiculous tripe coming from an administration that spent more that $70,000 on just printing the 2000-plus page monolith.  It's beyond a joke. Since when did liberals care about being fiscally responsible?

As Rush Limbaugh put it: "Now, anybody with independent intelligence, anybody who doesn't just follow conventional wisdom has to know that the ObamaCare budget busts like crazy. There is zilch, zero, nada deficit reduction with ObamaCare."

Don’t want to take Limbaugh's word for it. How about The Heritage Foundation: "The new law includes several new taxes and penalties for businesses that threaten to kill job growth and further damage the economy. Budget gimmicks and double counting of savings mean ObamaCare will increase federal deficit spending significantly."

Nancy Pelosi, recently announced that her caucus would focus on reducing the deficit and creating jobs. Well, seeing as how she seems so desperate to reduce the deficit... Madame Pelosi we look forward to your support of the REPEAL of Obama’s job killing, deficit-ballooning, socialized health care pipe dream.

Enough with the spin, enough with the lies, enough with the round the clock lying. The American people want ObamaCare repealed. The American people want it repealedNOW. We must push our advantage and take the battle straight to Barack Obama’s desk. We will REPEAL ObamaCare once and for all. It CAN be done but our elected officials must hear from you today.
