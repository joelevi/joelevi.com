Nullification: Last Chance To Stop ObamaCare?

January 28, 2011 · Articles

Stand Up For State Rights & Urge Congress To Nullify ObamaCare

ALERT: Right now, eight states are pushing to nullify ObamaCare. Maine, Montana, Oregon, Nebraska, Texas, Oklahoma and Wyoming, and Idaho are presenting bills this week that would make the president’s signature on the reform law void.

Nullification is a legal theory that says states have the right to nullify, or invalidate, any federal law which that state has deemed unconstitutional. The theory is based on the fact that sovereign states form the Union.

As members of the Union, the states have final authority regarding the limits of the power of the central government. There has never been a federal law that is more unconstitutional than ObamaCare.

The process of nullification has been used before. Back in 1799, Thomas Jefferson wrote the "Kentucky Resolution," responding to a series of federal laws passed during an undeclared naval war against France. Jefferson said, "Nullification, by those sovereignties, of all unauthorized acts... is the rightful remedy."

Last Wednesday, the House of Representatives voted to repeal ObamaCare. However, the healthcare law repeal has yet to pass the Senate. If it does make it through the Senate, Obama can still veto the law. Nullification is the last chance we have to get rid of ObamaCare.

ObamaCare Lawsuit: Model For Nullification

Just days after ObamaCare was signed into law, Florida’s former Attorney General Bill McCollum (R) filed a lawsuit against the individual mandate. The National Federal of Independent Business joins McCollum in fighting the trillion dollar budget buster.

A total of 27 states are taking part in the lawsuit – that’s more than half the states in the Union! If that isn’t enough to tell the Dems “No” to ObamaCare, I don’t know what is.

Sen. Monty Pierce, (R-ID) plans on introducing the nullification bill in his state this week. He said, “There are now 27 states that are in on the lawsuit against Obamacare. What if those 27 states do the same thing we do with nullification? It's a killer."
